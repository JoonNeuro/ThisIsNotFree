Use case: logo-brand. Design a single square macOS app icon artwork for This Is Not Free, a generous free utility collection with an ironic name. Concept: the dignified portrait on an imaginary ZERO-value banknote. A completely fictional illustrious elderly benefactor, looking like a revered philosopher or inventor, three-quarter bust portrait, high forehead, swept-back wavy hair, neat short beard, formal high-collared coat, calm intelligent eyes and a very subtle knowing smile. Gravitas and generosity, genuinely distinguished like the celebrated person engraved on a banknote. Do not depict any identifiable actual historical person. Fine authentic intaglio engraving with extremely precise curved contour hatching, dense cross-hatching and stippled transitions, meticulous linework in the face and clothes. All marks dark forest-green ink on warm ivory paper; restrained single-ink banknote palette, no photorealistic shading, color painting or cartoon. Portrait occupies the central 70 percent, framed by a refined engraved oval and a restrained guilloche ornamental border. A large engraved numeral '0' in each of the upper-left and lower-right corners clearly establishes the zero denomination. ONLY the numerals '0', no other text, no real country name, real currency title, serial number, watermark, seals or logos. Strong central head silhouette and clean hierarchy for an app icon, fine details reward looking closely. Tight square composition filling the canvas edge-to-edge, no rounded icon container, no device mockup, no drop shadow. This is original fictional banknote-inspired icon art.
